# Lantern journey

A party's wagon passage along a fictional trail to Lantern Harbor.

## Language

**Journey**: One passage from Briar Landing to Lantern Harbor, ending in arrival or failure.
_Avoid_: Run, level

**Party**: The four named travelers sharing a wagon; deceased members remain in the party record.
_Avoid_: Crew, lives

**Travel day**: One day spent advancing along the route, paying food and wagon wear.
_Avoid_: Tick, turn

**Camp day**: A day spent resting, foraging, or repairing without advancing.
_Avoid_: Free recovery

**River gate**: A river landmark that must be resolved before passage can continue.
_Avoid_: Minigame

**Settlement**: A route landmark where supplies can be purchased until departure.
_Avoid_: Shop level

**Lantern**: The emblem of the party's shared destination and hope.
_Avoid_: Currency
